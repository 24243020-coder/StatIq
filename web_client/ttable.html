Enter df to highlight that row. Click any cell to see its critical value.

How to use this table

This table shows critical t values for common significance levels (α).
Example: df = 10, two-tailed α = 0.05 → critical t = 2.228
If your calculated |t| exceeds the critical value, you reject H₀ at that significance level.
One-tailed α = half the two-tailed α (e.g. one-tailed α = 0.025 same as two-tailed α = 0.05).
